## 1. Central Bank Communications & Policy Tracker

### Federal Reserve Board – United States

**Regulatory and Supervisory Developments**

The Federal Reserve Board has initiated a significant regulatory consultation on payment stablecoin oversight. On 24 September, the Board requested public comment on two proposals establishing a regulatory framework for Board-supervised payment stablecoin issuers under the GENIUS Act [News: Federal Reserve Board | Date: Thu, 24 Sep 2026 18:30:00 GMT]. This represents a substantive step toward formalizing the regulatory treatment of digital payment instruments and clarifying supervisory expectations for entities engaged in stablecoin issuance activities. The consultation period signals the Board's commitment to developing a comprehensive oversight regime for this emerging class of financial intermediaries.

**Supervisory Actions**

The Board maintained active supervision during the reporting period, approving applications from two regional banking organizations. On 22 September, the Board approved an application by BancFirst Corporation [News: Federal Reserve Board | Date: Tue, 22 Sep 2026 20:30:00 GMT], followed by approval of an application by Peoples Bancorp Inc. on 25 September [News: Federal Reserve Board | Date: Fri, 25 Sep 2026 20:30:00 GMT]. These approvals reflect ongoing consolidation activity within the regional banking sector.

The Board also executed enforcement measures against individuals. On 24 September, an enforcement action was issued against a former employee of Sandy Spring Bank [News: Federal Reserve Board | Date: Thu, 24 Sep 2026 15:00:00 GMT]. Earlier in the week, on 18 September, the Board issued enforcement actions involving former employees of three institutions: Northstar Bank, American Express Travel Related Services Company, Inc., and Regions Bank [News: Federal Reserve Board | Date: Fri, 18 Sep 2026 15:00:00 GMT]. The pattern of individual-level enforcement actions underscores continued supervisory focus on conduct risk and accountability frameworks within supervised institutions.
